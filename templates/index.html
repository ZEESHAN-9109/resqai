The problem

After a disaster, the hardest task is knowing where to go first.

Imagery pours in from drones, satellites and the field faster than any team can triage. Damage is uncertain, routes change by the minute, and every decision must be defensible. RESQAI organises that flood of information into verifiable operational intelligence — without pretending to replace inspectors on the ground.

The solution

One workspace from raw imagery to assigned responders.

Live disaster intelligence

Real-time USGS earthquakes and GDACS / NASA EONET active fires, each stamped with source, freshness and data age. No fabricated events.

Image analysis

Vision AI flags potential damaged buildings, blocked roads and service disruptions with calibrated confidence.

Human verification

Responders confirm, correct or reject each finding. Nothing is ever auto-marked as verified.

Priority zones

Vulnerability and accessibility are combined with evidence — priority is never AI confidence alone.

Evidence traceability

Every recommendation carries a timeline of the images, reports and verifications behind it.

Response & routing

Assign responders, track status and plan routes with honest, evidence-based accessibility.

Live intelligence

Truthful, sourced, time-stamped.

Every external data item shows its source, timestamp and age. When a feed is unavailable we say so — we never silently substitute fake data. An earthquake event alone never implies building damage: damage only appears when real analysis and evidence exist.

USGS Earthquakes

Magnitude, depth, location and coordinates from the public USGS feed.

GDACS / NASA EONET

Free public active-fire alerts with no paid API key required.

OpenStreetMap

Free base mapping. Google Maps is supported as an optional add-on.

Responsible AI

AI assists responders. It does not replace them.

AI-generated findings are decision-support signals and must be verified against available evidence and official field assessment. RESQAI makes no claim of guaranteed detection or fully autonomous response.
